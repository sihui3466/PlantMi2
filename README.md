# PlantMi2 1D Project

Our android app is a plant care and watering app.

Problem:

A significant number of individuals reside in urban settings where access to expansive natural spaces is limited. Cultivating plants has emerged as a popular pastime among young adults seeking to incorporate nature into their indoor living spaces. However, due to their hectic schedules, many struggle to provide the frequent and constant necessary care for their plants. Consequently, we identified the following challenge:  

How might we help urban working adults keep their plants healthy and thriving despite their busy schedules? 

Our proposed solution is an app named PLANT MI. This app is a plant-care application which allows busy plant-enthusiasts to water and monitor their house plants, even while away from home. This is done by incorporating a set of sensors to each plant and a water container, which PLANT MI will be able to communicate with. These are implemented in a way that makes it easy for users to apply to any house plant and is compatible with any water container.
